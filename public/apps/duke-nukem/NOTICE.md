# Duke Nukem — shareware Episode 1 (Shrapnel City)

Apogee Software, 1991.

## Why this is hosted here

Episode 1 is the freely-distributable shareware episode. The included VENDOR.DOC permits distributing it and explicitly forbids distributing any registered version or any episode other than #1. Episodes 2 and 3 are NOT included.
