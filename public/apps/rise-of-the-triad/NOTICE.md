# Rise of the Triad: The HUNT Begins — shareware

Apogee Software, 1995.

## Why this is hosted here

The HUNT Begins is the freely-distributable shareware episode. The included VENDOR.DOC permits distributing the shareware version (no written permission is needed for on-line distribution) and forbids distributing any registered version. The registered game, Rise of the Triad: Dark War, is NOT included; it is still sold.
