# Commander Keen 4: Secret of the Oracle — shareware episode

id Software / Apogee Software, 1991

## Why this is hosted here

The official free shareware episode of Goodbye Galaxy. Episode 5 (The Armageddon Machine) and Keen 6 are commercial and are NOT included — the series is sold as the Commander Keen Complete Pack on Steam.
