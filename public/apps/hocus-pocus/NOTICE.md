# Hocus Pocus — shareware Episode 1

Moonlite Software / Apogee Software, 1994.

## Why this is hosted here

Episode 1 is the freely-distributable shareware episode. The included
VENDOR.DOC permits distributing the shareware episode unmodified and in its
entirety, and explicitly forbids distributing "any version that is registered,
or not marked shareware, or that contains any episode other than #1" — so only
Episode 1 is here.

Episodes 2 to 4 were the commercial purchase and are NOT included. The full
game is still sold on Steam and GOG.

## Source
