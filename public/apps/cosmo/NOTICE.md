# Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure — shareware Episode 1

Apogee Software, 1992. Episode 1, "Forbidden Planet".

## Why this is hosted here

Episode 1 is the freely-distributable shareware episode. Apogee's VENDOR.DOC
(included in the payload) permits distribution of the shareware episode
unmodified and in its entirety, and explicitly forbids distributing any
registered or non-shareware version — so only Episode 1 is here.

Episodes 2 and 3 were the commercial purchase and are NOT included. The
trilogy is still sold on Steam and GOG.

## Source

Retrieved 2026-07-29 from DOS Games Archive:
https://www.dosgamesarchive.com/download/cosmos-cosmic-adventure
(pre-installed "COSMO box" package.)

Original 1992 game files plus a `.jsdos/dosbox.conf` DOSBox configuration.
Modern additions from the source package (icons, BBS/catalog/dealer
executables, launcher batch files, info card) were removed; VENDOR.DOC and
ORDER.FRM are retained as the licence requires.
