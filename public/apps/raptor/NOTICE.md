# Raptor: Call of the Shadows — shareware episode

Cygnus Studios / Apogee Software, 1994

## Why this is hosted here

Bravo Sector is the official free shareware episode distributed under Apogee's shareware model. Tango and Outer Regions sectors are commercial and are NOT included — the full game is sold on Steam and GOG.

## Source

Retrieved 2026-07-28 from DOS Games Archive: https://www.dosgamesarchive.com/download/raptor-call-of-the-shadows

Only original game files plus a `.jsdos/dosbox.conf` DOSBox configuration are
included; installers, icons, PDFs and help launchers were removed.
