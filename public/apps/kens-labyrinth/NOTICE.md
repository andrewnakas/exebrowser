# Ken's Labyrinth — complete freeware release

Ken Silverman / Epic MegaGames, 1993. Released free by the author on November 16, 1999.

## Why this is hosted here

From the included README.TXT: Ken Silverman, with permission from Epic MegaGames, released the full version of Ken's Labyrinth for free; "You may copy this software freely", provided no files from the original archive are removed and it is not sold. Every file of his archive is included, unmodified.
