# Duke Nukem II — shareware Episode 1 (Neutron Fallout)

An Apogee Game. Apogee Software, 1993.

## Why this is hosted here

Episode 1 is the shareware version, distributed under the included LICENSE.DOC, which licenses distribution of the shareware version only, marked as shareware (try before you buy) and described as An Apogee Game. Episodes 2 to 4 are NOT included.
