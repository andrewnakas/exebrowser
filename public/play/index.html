Browse by category

The catalogue is small enough to scroll and varied enough that scrolling isn't much help. These pages group it by what a game actually is — not by the decade it came from or the engine it runs on, both of which matter to us and to nobody else.

Every title on every one of these pages is hosted here and boots in the tab you're reading this in. Nothing asks for a download, an account, or a copy of the original you're expected to already own; the software that does need your own copy lives in the compatibility guides instead, clearly marked.

  • Classic shooters, playable in your browserEight DOS-era shooters running free in your browser — DOOM, Wolfenstein 3D, Blake Stone, Raptor, Tyrian and more. No download, no install, no account.
  • Side-scrolling platformers, playable in your browserTen DOS platformers running free in your browser — Commander Keen, Cosmo, Crystal Caves, Xargon, Monster Bash, Bio Menace and more. Instant, no download.
  • The Windows desktop classics, playable in your browserSolitaire, Minesweeper, FreeCell, Spider, Hearts, JezzBall, SkiFree and 3D Pinball — the games that shipped with Windows, playable free in your browser.
  • Card games, playable in your browserKlondike Solitaire, FreeCell, Spider Solitaire and Hearts — the four Windows card games, rebuilt as open implementations. Free, instant, no sign-up.

Not every filter has a page of its own — a category with one or two titles in it makes a thin page, so those stay as filters on the full catalogue instead.
