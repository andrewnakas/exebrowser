Free games you can put on your own site

These 11 games were written from scratch for this site rather than emulated, which means they are ours to give away — and we do. Copy one <iframe>, paste it into your page, done. No API key, no sign-up, no advertising, no tracking, no fee.

They are small enough not to matter: the largest is 48 KB, less than a single photograph, and the frame loads lazily so nothing is fetched until somebody scrolls to it. There is no framework underneath and no third-party player phoning home.

The only condition is the credit line that comes with the snippet. Leave it in place and you are square with us.

What the snippet does

Each block above is complete and self-contained — an iframe pointing at a dedicated wrapper page, plus the credit paragraph. The wrapper is deliberately bare: no navigation, no header, no advertising, just the game filling the frame.

Adjust width and height to suit your layout. The default is full width up to 760px and 600px tall, which suits most article columns; the games are responsive and will fill whatever you give them. loading="lazy" is in there on purpose and worth keeping.

Want to see one in isolation first? Every entry above links to its bare frame, which is exactly what your visitors will get.

Common questions

Can I really put these on my own site for free?

Yes, for these 11. They were written from scratch here rather than emulated, so they are ours to hand on, and you need no permission and no key. Keeping the credit line under the frame is the only thing we ask.

Why aren't DOOM and the other classics on this list?

Because they aren't ours to give. We host them under shareware and freeware licences that let us run them on this site, which is not the same as a right to sub-license them to anybody else. Offering them for embedding would be handing out permission we don't have.

Will this slow my page down?

Very little. The iframe loads lazily, so nothing is fetched until a visitor scrolls it into view, and every game here is small — the largest is 48 KB, which is less than one photograph. There is no framework and no third-party player.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Every game has touch controls and the frame is responsive, so it fills whatever width you give it up to 760px. Set your own width and height on the iframe if that suits your layout better.

Do you track my visitors?

The embedded frame carries no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. It is the game and nothing else. We can't see who plays it on your site and we don't want to.

Will the game break if you change something?

The embed URLs are stable and we treat them as an interface, not an internal detail. If a game ever had to be withdrawn, the URL would keep working and show a short message rather than turning into a broken frame on your page.

The rest of the catalogue

Beyond the 11 above, this site runs the real DOS and Windows originals — DOOM, Wolfenstein 3D, SkiFree, Tyrian and a few dozen more — through emulators compiled to WebAssembly. Those we host under their own shareware and freeware licences, so they are playable here but not ours to hand on for embedding.
