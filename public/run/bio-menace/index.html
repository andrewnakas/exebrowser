How it works & what to expect

The original DOS release runs in a WebAssembly DOSBox build on this page. The title sequence leads into a menu — press Esc if you land in the ordering-info screens, which is easy to do by mashing Enter. Episode One, Dr. Mangle's Lab, is included.

Playing tips

Crouch while shooting to hit low enemies — you'll need it constantly. If you land in the ordering-info screens by pressing Enter through the title sequence, press Esc to get back out.

Why it's free

3D Realms released Bio Menace as freeware in December 2005 as part of a batch of legacy Apogee titles. It's the same complete game they distributed, not a cut-down demo.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bio Menace legal to play here?

Yes. 3D Realms released it as freeware in 2005, so this build is legal to host, play and share.

I ended up in an ordering-info screen — how do I get out?

Press Esc. The title sequence sits close to the order pages, and pressing Enter repeatedly walks into them.

What are the controls?

Arrows move and duck, Ctrl jumps, Alt fires. Crouching while shooting is essential against low enemies.

Does my progress save?

Yes — saved games persist in this browser between visits, best-effort.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes, a d-pad with JUMP and FIRE appears on touch devices.
