How it works & what to expect

The original DOS shareware release runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It is about 300 KB and starts almost instantly. The first screen shows the sound and graphics setup; press Enter to accept it.

It runs at full speed on anything, including phones and Chromebooks.

Playing it

Arrow keys move, Shift jumps and Alt throws your knife once you have found one — it comes back like a boomerang. The episode is built around a hub map: walk to a level's entrance and press up to go in. Keys open the matching doors, and apples restore health.

Shareware version — and the full game

This is the first of three episodes, the one Epic MegaGames released as shareware. Its VENDOR.DOC grants permission to distribute it as long as it is clearly identified as shareware. The other two episodes are not hosted here.

Frequently asked questions

Is Jill of the Jungle free to play here?

Yes. This is the shareware first episode, which Epic MegaGames gave permission to distribute in the VENDOR.DOC included in the package.

How many episodes are included?

One of three. Episodes two (Jill Goes Underground) and three (Jill Saves the Prince) were the registered part of the game.

Who made it?

Tim Sweeney programmed it for his company Epic MegaGames, which later became Epic Games.

Does my progress save?

Yes, best-effort. Games saved from Jill's own menu are kept in this browser.
