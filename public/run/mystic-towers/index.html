Mystic Towers is the 1994 isometric action-puzzle game by the Australian studio Animation F/X, published by Apogee. Baron Baldric, an elderly wizard with a bad back, has to clear towers of monsters room by room, casting spells and remembering to eat and drink. How it works & what to expect

The original DOS shareware release runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. The package is about 1 MB. Press Enter at the opening text screens to reach the game. It runs at full speed on any recent device.

Playing it

The arrow keys walk Baldric around each isometric room. Jumping, casting and picking things up are on the keyboard; the game's help screen lists the keys. In each tower you must kill every monster, destroy the monster generator and find the key to the exit. Watch the food and drink meters: an adventurer who forgets lunch does not last.

Shareware version — and the full game

This is the shareware release, distributed under Apogee's VENDOR.DOC, which is included in the package. It contains the first towers; the registered game has the rest and is not hosted here.
