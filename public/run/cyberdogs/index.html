How it works & what to expect

The original DOS game runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It is about 260 KB and starts in a couple of seconds. It runs at full speed on any device.

Playing it

Choose one or two players from the menu, then spend your starting cash in the shop: a second weapon and some armour go a long way. In each mission, complete the objectives shown at the start, then get to the exit. The keys for both players are shown, and can be changed, in the game's options.

Why it is free

Cyberdogs is freeware. The author's README says: “This game is freeware, feel free to spread it around.” The files here are the unmodified release.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cyberdogs really free?

Yes. Its author, Ronny Wester, released it as freeware and invites people to spread it around. This is the complete game.

Can two people play together?

Yes, on one keyboard, co-operatively. Each player's keys are set in the options menu.

How is it related to C-Dogs?

Cyberdogs came first. Wester's sequel, C-Dogs, followed in 1997, and the open-source C-Dogs SDL project continues it today.
