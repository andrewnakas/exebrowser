Play Neverball online — free, in your browser ▶ Play now — official web build, runs right here

Guide updated November 2026 · Runs via WebAssembly (native port)

Neverball is the open-source game where you do not move the ball — you tilt the floor under it. Roll through each 3D course, collect enough coins to open the goal, and get there before time runs out. This is the Neverball project's own official web build. Press Play Now in the launcher below.

How it works & what to expect

The game is the official “Neverball in the browser” build, compiled to WebAssembly. About 28 MB of game data downloads the first time, then the Play Now button becomes active. It needs WebGL, which every current browser has, and runs well on an ordinary laptop or a recent Chromebook.

Playing it

Move the mouse, or use the arrow keys, to tilt the floor. Left-click and right-click rotate the camera. On a touch screen, swipe to tilt and use a second thumb for the camera; a gamepad works too. Each level shows how many coins the goal needs; collect that many and the goal opens. Falling off the edge costs you a ball.

Start with the Easy set. The later sets are much harder than they first look.

Frequently asked questions

Is Neverball free?

Yes. It is free software under the GNU GPL, and this is the complete game, not a demo.

Is Neverball unblocked here?

Nothing is installed: the game is files served from this site. Whether a particular school or office network allows this site is up to that network. The game's Addons menu asks the official Neverball site for its add-on list; the game plays without it.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Scores, unlocked levels and replays are stored in this browser.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. Swipe to tilt the floor. A larger screen is more comfortable.
