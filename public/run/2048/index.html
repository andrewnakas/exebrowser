How to play

Use the arrow keys, or swipe on a touch screen. Every move slides all the tiles as far as they will go in that direction. When two tiles with the same number meet, they merge into one tile with their sum. After each move a new 2 or 4 appears in an empty square. You win when you make a 2048 tile, and you can keep going after that; the game ends when the board is full and no move is possible.

Getting past 512

Pick a corner and keep your largest tile there. Fill that tile's row in descending order, and avoid the one direction that would pull the big tile out of its corner unless you have no other move. Most games are lost by a single careless swipe, not by bad luck.

Why it is free

Cirulli published 2048 under the MIT licence, which allows anyone to copy and host it. The files here are his, unmodified, and the game itself makes no requests to any other site. Your best score is stored in your own browser.
