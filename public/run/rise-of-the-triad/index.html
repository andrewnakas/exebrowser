How it works & what to expect

The original DOS shareware release (1.3) running in a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. The package is under 4 MB. The game itself caps at 35 frames a second, which a recent laptop or desktop holds; slower devices will drop below it.

Playing tips

Each of the five characters trades speed against health — Taradino is the all-rounder. Missile weapons are picked up and used until they run out, after which you fall back to your pistols. Jump pads and moving platforms hide most of the secrets.

Shareware episode — and the full game

The HUNT Begins is the shareware episode Apogee distributed; its VENDOR.DOC permits sharing it and forbids distributing the registered game, so only the shareware is here. The full game, Rise of the Triad: Dark War, is still sold.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rise of the Triad legal to play here?

Yes. This is Apogee's official shareware episode, distributed under its VENDOR.DOC, the same basis as the Cosmo, Hocus Pocus and Blake Stone episodes hosted here.

What are the controls?

Arrow keys move, Ctrl fires, Space opens doors, Shift runs and Alt strafes by default. The controls can be changed under Options in the game's menu.

It is just showing credits — is it broken?

No. The game shows its credits and plays a demo when left alone. Press any key to get the menu.
