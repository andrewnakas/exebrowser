Playing tips

Wind is shown top-right and changes every round — reading it is most of the skill. Between rounds you spend winnings in the shop, which is where the game really opens up; the AI's higher difficulty levels have real personality.

Shareware — and buying it

Scorched Earth was distributed as shareware, and author Wendell Hicken's site whicken.com has hosted it for decades. If you enjoy it, the registration the game asks about is the author's own.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scorched Earth free to play here?

It's the shareware release, distributed freely by its author for over thirty years. The in-game registration prompts are the author's original ones.

What are the controls?

Left/Right aim, Up/Down set power, Space fires, Tab cycles weapons, Esc opens the menu. Watch the wind indicator — it changes every round.

How many players can play?

Up to ten tanks per battle, in any mix of humans and computer opponents. Hotseat multiplayer works on one keyboard.

Does my progress save?

Settings and scores persist in this browser between visits, best-effort.
