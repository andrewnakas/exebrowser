How it works & what to expect

This is the original 1990 DOS shareware release (v1.31 data, real KEEN1.EXE) running inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox — not a remake or source port. The package is tiny (~150 KB), so it boots in a couple of seconds. The title loop cycles through the story and high scores on its own; press Enter to bring up the menu and choose New Game.

Playing tips

The pogo stick is the whole game once you find it — hold Ctrl while bouncing to reach ledges a normal jump can't. Yorps are harmless if you walk past them; the Garg is not. On the Mars overworld, walk onto a level entrance to enter it.

Shareware episode — and the rest of the trilogy

Marooned on Mars is Episode 1 of the Invasion of the Vorticons trilogy, released free under Apogee's shareware model — sharing it was literally the marketing plan. Episodes 2 and 3 (The Earth Explodes, Keen Must Die!) are still commercial and are not hosted here; the whole series is sold cheaply on Steam as the Commander Keen Complete Pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to play Commander Keen here?

Yes. Episode 1 was released by id Software and Apogee as freely-redistributable shareware in 1990, and that license still stands. Episodes 2 and 3 are commercial and not included — buy the Complete Pack on Steam for those.

Is this the original game or a remake?

The original — the real 1990 KEEN1.EXE and game data (v1.31) running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly, right in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or installed.

How do I use the pogo stick?

Press Alt to toggle the pogo once you've collected it in a level (one early level hides it). Hold jump (Ctrl) while bouncing to bounce higher. Half the game's secrets need pogo bounces to reach.

Does my progress save?

Yes — progress now persists. Saved games and high scores are kept in your browser and restored automatically next time you open this page. It's per-browser and best-effort: clearing site data, or a long gap on iOS, can drop it. Use the “reset saved progress” link under the game to start over deliberately.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes — a touch pad with a d-pad, JUMP, and POGO buttons appears under the game on touch devices, though a keyboard is the classic way to play.
