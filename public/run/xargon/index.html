How it works & what to expect

The original DOS release runs in a WebAssembly DOSBox on this page. On first boot Xargon asks whether you want digital sound — answer Y and it configures itself. Levels reward poking at scenery; a lot of the map is optional and hidden.

Look for breakable blocks and false walls — Xargon hides a great deal behind them, and the secret areas hold the interesting items. On first boot the game asks about digital sound; press Y and it configures itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is Xargon free?

Yes. Author Allen Pilgrim released the full trilogy as freeware in 2008 under an explicit written licence, and also released the source code.

It's asking about digital sound on startup — what do I pick?

Press Y for digital sound through Sound Blaster. If audio misbehaves, reload and answer N to fall back to simpler sound.

What are the controls?

Arrows move, Ctrl jumps, Alt fires, Esc opens the menu. Search for breakable blocks and false walls — much of each level is hidden.

Does my progress save?

Yes, saved games persist in this browser between visits, best-effort.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes — a d-pad with JUMP and FIRE appears on touch devices, plus a keyboard palette for the setup prompts.
