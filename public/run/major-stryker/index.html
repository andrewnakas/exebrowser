How it works & what to expect

Apogee's own freeware package runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It is a little over 1 MB. The game is from the 16-colour EGA era and runs at full speed on any device.

Playing it

The arrow keys move your ship. The fire key and the others are shown in the game's instructions and can be changed from its menu. Pick up the weapon pods that freed scientists and destroyed targets leave behind, and try not to get hit: a hit costs you your upgraded gun before it costs you a life.
