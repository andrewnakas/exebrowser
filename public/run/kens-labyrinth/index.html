Ken's Labyrinth was released free in full by its author, Ken Silverman, in 1999 with Epic MegaGames' permission. Every file of his release is included. Provenance: /apps/kens-labyrinth/NOTICE.md.

How it works & what to expect

The original DOS release running in a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. The whole game is under 1 MB. Every file from the author's release is included, as his README asks.

Playing tips

Fireballs are your first weapon and they bounce — fire around corners. Keys and coins are everywhere; the coins buy things from the vending machines on later floors. Press Space on doors to open them.

Why the whole game is free

Epic MegaGames published it as shareware. When Epic stopped selling its older titles, Silverman asked, and released the full version for free in November 1999. His only conditions are to keep every file of his archive together and not to sell it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ken's Labyrinth legal to play here?

Yes. Its author released the complete game for free in 1999 with Epic MegaGames' permission, asking only that every file stays in the package — which it does.

What are the controls?

Arrow keys move and turn, left Ctrl shoots, Space opens doors, left Shift runs and right Ctrl strafes. F1 selects the fireball weapon.
