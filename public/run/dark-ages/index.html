How it works & what to expect

Apogee's own freeware package runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It is under 400 KB and loads at once. It is a 16-colour EGA game and runs at full speed on any device.

The page starts volume one. Volumes two and three are in the same package: quit the game to the DOS prompt and type DA2 or DA3.

Playing it

The arrow keys move the prince; the jump and fire keys are shown in the game's instructions. Your weapon improves as you find upgrades, and shields protect you for a limited number of hits. Talk to the wise man in each level, who will trade you a clue or an item for what he asks.
