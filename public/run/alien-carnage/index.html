Frequently asked questions

Is Alien Carnage free?

Yes. Apogee released it as freeware in 2007 together with original author John Passfield, so it's legal to host and play in full.

Is this the same game as Halloween Harry?

Yes. It was originally released as Halloween Harry and later renamed Alien Carnage for the Apogee release.

What are the controls?

Arrows move, Ctrl jumps and holds for the jetpack, Alt fires the flamethrower. Jetpack fuel is limited and refills from pickups.
