Math Rescue is the 1992 educational platformer by Karen Crowther's Redwood Games, published by Apogee. Creatures called Gruzzles have stolen the world's numbers, and you win them back by solving arithmetic problems while a helpful butterfly drops slime on anything that gets in your way. How it works & what to expect

The original DOS shareware release runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It is under 400 KB and loads at once. It runs at full speed on any device, including school Chromebooks.

Playing it

Choose a boy or a girl and a difficulty level; the levels set which kinds of problems appear, from simple addition up to multiplication and division, including word problems. Arrow keys move and Ctrl jumps. Touch a number to get a problem, answer it, and collect the answers to open the exit.

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to play Math Rescue here?

Yes. This is the official shareware episode, which Apogee's VENDOR.DOC allows to be passed on without charge.

What ages is it for?

It was made for primary-school children. The difficulty setting changes the arithmetic from simple sums to multiplication, division and word problems.

Does it work on a school Chromebook?

Yes. Nothing is installed and the game is light enough for any Chromebook.
