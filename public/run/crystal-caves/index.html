How it works & what to expect

The original 1991 DOS release, running in a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It's tiny — under 200 KB — so it boots almost instantly. There's a story intro before the menu (Mylo's ship steering fails again); press Space through it to reach the level select.

Playing tips

Every crystal on a level must be collected before the exit will let you leave, so sweep the whole map rather than heading for the door. Your pistol has unlimited ammo but a slow rate of fire — shoot from a distance and keep moving. The floating platforms and conveyor floors carry you, so use them rather than fighting them, and watch for the stalactites that drop when you walk underneath.

Shareware episode — and the full trilogy

Episode 1 is the free shareware episode Apogee distributed. Its VENDOR.DOC permits sharing that episode and forbids distributing any registered version, so only Episode 1 is here. The full trilogy is still sold — look for Crystal Caves on Steam or GOG.

Frequently asked questions

Is Crystal Caves legal to play here?

Yes. This is the official shareware Episode 1, distributed freely by Apogee under its shareware model — the same basis as the Commander Keen, Cosmo and Hocus Pocus episodes we host. Episodes 2 and 3 are commercial and are not included.

What are the controls?

Arrow keys move, Ctrl jumps, Alt fires your pistol. Esc opens the menu. The full control table is under the game.

Why won't the exit work?

You need every crystal on the level first. If the exit seems inert, there's at least one crystal you haven't reached — often behind a conveyor or up a set of floating platforms.

There's a long intro before the menu — is it stuck?

No, that's the game's story sequence. Press Space a few times to skip through it to the level select.

Does my progress save?

Yes — saved games and high scores persist in this browser between visits, best-effort.
