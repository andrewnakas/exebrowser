Playing tips

Shareware episode — and the rest

Secret of the Oracle was the free shareware episode of the Goodbye Galaxy pair. Episode 5, The Armageddon Machine, and Keen 6 are commercial — the whole series is sold as the Commander Keen Complete Pack on Steam.

Frequently asked questions

Is Commander Keen 4 free to play here?

This is the official shareware episode, which id Software and Apogee distributed free. Episodes 5 and 6 are commercial and not included — buy the Complete Pack on Steam for those.

How does the pogo stick work?

Press Alt to pogo, and hold Ctrl while bouncing to jump much higher. Most secret areas are only reachable with a high pogo bounce.

What are the controls?

Arrows move, Ctrl jumps, Alt pogos, Esc opens the menu, F1 shows in-game help.

Does my progress save?

Yes — Keen 4 has a proper save system and saves persist in this browser between visits, best-effort.

How is this different from Commander Keen 1?

Keen 4 is bigger and more polished: a world map, a save system, better level design, and the pogo stick as a core mechanic rather than a curiosity.
