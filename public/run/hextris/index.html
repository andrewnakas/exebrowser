Play Hextris online — the hexagon puzzle game ▶ Play now — runs right here in your browser

Guide updated November 2026 · Runs via WebAssembly (native port)

Hextris is a fast puzzle game built around a hexagon. Coloured blocks fall towards it from all six sides; you rotate the hexagon so they land where you want them. Join three or more of the same colour and they clear. Four students wrote it in 2014 and released it as open source, and it runs right here.

How to play

Press the left and right arrow keys to rotate the hexagon, or tap the left and right sides of the screen. Holding the down arrow speeds the blocks up. Blocks clear when three or more of one colour touch, and clearing several groups in a row builds a combo. The game ends when a stack on any side grows past the grey outer hexagon.

Lasting longer

Keep the six stacks level instead of clearing one side at a time: a tall stack is what ends the game. Look at the colour of the next block before you rotate, and when two blocks are falling at once, deal with the nearer one first.
