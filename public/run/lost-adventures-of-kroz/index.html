About The Lost Adventures of Kroz

The Lost Adventures gathers new levels in the same text-mode style as the rest of the series, with every object the earlier games introduced, from lava and quakes to invisible walls. The hint program in the folder, KROZHINT, helps with the trickiest screens. It is the place to go after finishing the shorter episodes.

How it runs here

This is the original DOS release of The Lost Adventures of Kroz, unmodified, running in DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The whole game is about 1 MB, so it starts within seconds on a normal connection. Sound and music play once you click into the game.
