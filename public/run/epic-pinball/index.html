How it works & what to expect

The original DOS shareware package runs inside a WebAssembly build of DOSBox. It is well under 1 MB, so it loads almost at once. The first screens are Epic's intro and a menu; press Enter to reach the table.

Epic Pinball was written in assembly for a 386, and it runs at full speed in the browser on nearly anything, including a Chromebook.

Playing it

The two Shift keys are the flippers. Hold the down arrow to pull the plunger back and let go to launch. Space nudges the table — do it too often and you tilt. The game's own help screen lists every key.

On the Android table, the first thing to learn is the ramp shots that spell out the robot's parts; completing the android is what starts the big scoring.

Frequently asked questions

Is Epic Pinball free to play here?

Yes. This is the official shareware release, which Epic MegaGames published for free distribution. The registered table packs are not hosted.

Which tables are included?

The shareware table, Android. The other tables were sold in the registered packs.

What are the controls?

Left and right Shift are the flippers, the down arrow pulls the plunger, and Space nudges the table.

Is this the Windows pinball game?

No. The pinball game that shipped with Windows was 3D Pinball: Space Cadet. This site has an open table for that engine on its own page; Epic Pinball is an earlier DOS game from a different studio.
